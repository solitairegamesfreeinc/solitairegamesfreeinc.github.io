What kind of app is it?

Spider Solitaire is about building order in a crowded tableau. Arrange cards into descending sequences, expose the cards underneath and work toward complete suited runs. Choosing which column to open can matter as much as the next available move: a useful empty column gives you room to reorganize cards that are blocking one another.

Spider Solitaire Card Games · supports tapping or dragging cards. Its iPhone and iPad listing describes unlimited games without a lives limit, so an unsuccessful deal does not use up a finite supply of attempts. Players comparing solitaire apps should also consider readable cards, suit options, undo, statistics and the way advertising interrupts a session.

The two store descriptions provide different levels of detail. Google Play explicitly lists one-, two- and four-suit games, numbered shuffles, unlimited undo, statistics and appearance options. Those Android features should not be assumed to be identical on iOS merely because the apps share a name.

Is Spider the same as Klondike solitaire?

No. Spider focuses on completing suited sequences across its tableau. Klondike uses a different deal, stock and foundation structure. Choose the ruleset you want rather than relying on the word solitaire alone.

Does every version have the same statistics and suit options?

The Android listing explicitly describes statistics and one-, two- and four-suit options. We have not verified equivalent detail for every iOS version.

Is the game completely free of ads?

Do not assume that. Google Play lists ads and purchases; the iOS description discusses an optional ad-free subscription. Check your store for the current offer, regional availability and renewal terms.

What kind of app is it?

Solitaire Word Associations puts complete words on cards and asks you to recognize how they belong together. Category-heading cards form the foundations: place word cards under the appropriate category and work toward clearing the board. The central task is understanding relationships between words, rather than spelling a word from individual letter cards.

The iPhone and iPad listing describes a move limit, stars for completing boards, hints, undo and offline play. A move limit changes the way you examine a position: an obvious category match can be useful, but it is also worth considering whether that move uncovers information or opens access to another card.

The app also advertises a story with mansion renovation. That is progression surrounding the category-card puzzle, rather than a different definition of word solitaire. The verified public product is Solitaire Word Associations from Solitaire Games Free Inc.; it should not be confused with unrelated apps that use similar word-solitaire names.

Before you download.

Do I spell words from individual letter cards?

No. The verified description is about placing complete word cards into categories. It is a word-association card puzzle.

Is this traditional Spider or Klondike solitaire?

No. It uses cards and foundations, but the organizing rule is a word’s category rather than the suits and ranks of a standard playing-card deck.

Can I play offline?

The iOS listing advertises offline play. We have not established that every surrounding service, purchase or progression feature works without a connection.

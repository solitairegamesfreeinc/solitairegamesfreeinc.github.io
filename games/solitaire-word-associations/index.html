A few things you might wonder.

Do I spell words from letter cards?

No. The cards already contain complete words; your task is to match them to the right categories.

Is there a move limit?

Yes. Complete the board within its move limit to earn stars.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Offline play is available on iPhone and iPad.
